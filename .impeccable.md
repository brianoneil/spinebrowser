# Spinebrowser — Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Power users and "Arc refugees" — people who loved Arc/Zen's sidebar-first, space-based browsing and want it on a dual-screen iPhone ("iPhone Duo"). Primary context: research and comparison workflows (search results on one screen, content on the other), reading, and multi-context browsing (work/personal spaces). They value craft, keyboard/gesture fluency, and a browser that stays out of the way. They are allergic to tab clutter and generic Chromium clones.

### Brand Personality
**Three words:** crafted, calm, alive.
- **Crafted** — Arc's cult following came from micro-affordances and motion; every detail must feel considered.
- **Calm** — chrome recedes, tabs clean themselves up, nothing nags. The interface should lower heart rate, not raise it.
- **Alive** — space-tinted gradients, spring motion, one signature moment of delight (tabs "shelving" into the archive).
- **Emotional goal:** the feeling of a tidy desk, not a productivity dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Tone:** refined minimalism with warm, tinted neutrals — never pure black/white, never cold gray. Arc-inspired gradients used with restraint (space identity, not decoration).
- **Color — "the reading room" (mineral mood).** Three layers in both modes: **cloth** (the Spine, tinted by the space's binding), **paper** (pages, sheets), **stone** (the desk behind everything). Day: desk #F6F3ED · page #FEFDFA · text #221D18 · secondary #605A54 · line #DEDAD3. Night: desk #14110E · page #1E1A16 · raised #292420 · text #EAE7E2 · secondary #A8A49E · line #342F2B. Night depth comes from lighter surfaces, never shadows; OLED true black is a reader-only option.
- **Meaningful colors only:** Verdigris #217969 / night #75BBAB = action, focus, selection (rare). Copper #8B4A1D / night #DA9B6A = time (Today countdown, archive). Madder #AD3C33 / night #E68677 = destructive + insecure, always with a word.
- **Bindings (space colors):** seven bookcloth hues — Verdigris (default), Lapis, Moss, Ochre, Madder, Damson, Graphite — each with day tint→deep wash, night tint/deep, ink, and its own secondary ink. Secondary text on cloth uses the binding's ink, never gray. A tonal wash within one hue, never a hue-shifting gradient. All text pairings verified ≥ 4.5:1.
- **Retired:** the v0.1 "chapel" amethyst palette and amethyst→rose gradients — too close to the AI-purple anti-reference.
- **References:** Arc (sidebar craft, gradients, chrome-less content), Zen (compact mode, floating URL bar), Apple HIG (materials, vibrancy, 44pt targets, SF Pro).
- **Anti-references:** generic mobile browsers with persistent top+bottom chrome bars; AI-slop aesthetics (cyan-on-dark, purple-blue neon gradients, glassmorphism everywhere); cluttered toolbar rows; modals for everything.
- **Themes:** light + dark, both first-class; true-black dark mode option for OLED. Space accent color tints the chrome — the space IS the theme.
- **Typography:** Two faces, two jobs. **SF Pro runs the interface** (Spine, bars, command, settings) on Apple text styles only: Large Title 34/41 Bold · Title 2 22/28 Bold · Headline 17/22 Semibold · Body 17/22 · Subheadline 15/20 (Spine tab rows) · Footnote 13/18 (URLs, countdowns) · Caption 2 11/13 Semibold caps +0.06em (section labels). **New York tells the story**: brand display 56/60 Semibold −0.02em, reader title 32/38, reader body 18/29 (18/30 on dark, 60–66ch, Aa 15–24), caption 13/18 italic. Presentation headings on design boards use New York too. Weights: 400 / 600 / 700 only — Medium retired. Tabular figures for countdowns, counts, page numbers. Nothing below 11pt; every UI size scales with Dynamic Type. Mocks stand in Inter for SF Pro and Source Serif 4 for New York.

### Design Principles
1. **The fold matters when it bends.** The inner display is one continuous canvas. Flat, layout ignores the crease. Half-folded (book, tabletop), the crease becomes the gutter and no text or tap target sits on it.
2. **Chrome recedes.** Content is full-bleed; UI appears on summon and decelerates out of the way. One persistent element max (the URL pill).
3. **Space color is identity.** Each space's gradient tints sidebar, pill, and accents — instant context recognition without reading a label.
4. **Motion decelerates, never bounces.** Spring physics (response ~0.4, damping ~0.8); ease-out-expo character; reduce-motion fully honored.
5. **Every posture is complete.** Flat, book, tabletop, closed — each gets its own layout. Moving between them keeps every tab, scroll position, and Pair (split).
6. **One Spine, many shapes.** Tabs live in one Spine that takes the form each posture needs: sidebar, rail, thumb index, console strip, or cover shelf.

### Accessibility
- WCAG AA contrast minimum in both themes; 44×44pt touch targets (HIG).
- Reduce Motion: springs collapse to crossfades; Reduce Transparency: materials fall back to solid tints.
- Dynamic Type support for all UI text; reader mode offers its own type sizing.
- Color is never the sole indicator (space identity also uses emoji/icon + label).
